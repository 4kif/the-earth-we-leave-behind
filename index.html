01 — UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM

OUR PLANET
IS CHANGING.

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperature, weather patterns and natural systems. Human activities have accelerated these changes.

🌡️

RISING TEMPERATURES

Earth's average temperature is increasing, affecting ecosystems, communities and natural systems.

🌊

RISING SEA LEVELS

Melting ice and warming oceans contribute to rising sea levels around the world.

🔥

EXTREME WEATHER

Climate change can increase risks from heatwaves, floods, droughts and intense storms.

🌳

LOSS OF NATURE

Changes in climate can disrupt habitats, ecosystems and biodiversity.

02 — THE CAUSES

WE ARE PART
OF THE PROBLEM.

Modern society depends heavily on activities that release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.

01

🏭 INDUSTRY

Industrial activities consume energy and produce greenhouse gas emissions.

02

🚗 TRANSPORT

Cars, trucks, ships and aircraft contribute to emissions through fossil fuel use.

03

🌳 DEFORESTATION

Destroying forests reduces the planet's ability to naturally absorb carbon dioxide.

04

⚡ ENERGY

Burning coal, oil and natural gas releases large amounts of greenhouse gases.

05

🌾 AGRICULTURE

Agriculture and livestock can contribute methane and other greenhouse gas emissions.

06

🗑️ WASTE

Poor waste management contributes to pollution and greenhouse gas emissions.
